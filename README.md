### Industrial Maintenance Management Platform · CMMS / GMAO

</div>

<table>
<tr>

### 💡 The Problem

Industrial maintenance operations often rely on:

* fragmented spreadsheets
* manual reporting
* disconnected data
* limited KPI visibility

**COFAT Smart Maintenance** aims to centralize these processes in a single digital platform.

</td>

A full-stack maintenance management platform providing:

* Work order management
* Maintenance history
* Reporting & analytics
* KPI dashboards
* Excel data processing
* Role-based access
* Notifications
